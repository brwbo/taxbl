Since 1 January 2026 UK exchanges have been recording your transactions for HMRC under CARF; the first reports land between January and May 2027. This tool reads the public chain instead, classifies every taxable event under HMRC's share-pooling rules (same-day, 30-day, Section 104), and shows your gains and income per tax year. Correct any classification and the numbers recalculate live.
