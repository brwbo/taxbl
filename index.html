What will HMRC ask you about your crypto?

taxtape turns a public Ethereum address into a UK tax position: every sale, swap, reward and transfer, matched under HMRC's own rules, totalled per tax year. It exists because most people holding crypto have never declared it, and from 2027 HMRC will know, and charge.

A public address lets anyone see history, not touch it. This page has no connect button and never asks you to sign anything. The address is sent to Blockscout, a public explorer, and nowhere else. We never see it, store it, or link it to you.
Verify it yourself: open DevTools → Network and watch where the address goes. Source: github.com/brwbo/taxtape

UK exchanges and custodial wallets started recording every user's sales, swaps and transfers under the OECD's CARF rules.

02 · Jan–May 2027

They file that data to HMRC. HMRC matches it against Self Assessment returns and writes to the people who never filed.

03 · The gap

The exchange file only shows what the exchange saw. What happened after the coins left is on the public ledger, and that is what you will have to reconcile.

How to use it

  1. Paste a public address. The one that starts 0x. Not a seed phrase, not a private key, no wallet connection. If you only use an exchange, try the sample to see what the output looks like.
  2. Check the guesses. Every event is classified (disposal, acquisition, income, transfer) and every guess is marked ⚠ with the reason. Change any you know to be wrong, such as a transfer to your own hardware wallet. The totals recalculate as you go.
  3. Read your position per tax year. Proceeds, allowable costs, gains, losses, the £3,000 exempt amount, what is taxable, and income from staking or airdrops. Then open the full report for the computation an accountant can file from.

Privacy notice

Who

taxtape is run by rowbo.ai (Ben Rowbotham), the data controller. Contact: ben@rowbo.ai.

The address you paste

Sent from your browser directly to Blockscout (a public block explorer) to read its history, and to Coinbase and Frankfurter (ECB) for daily prices. It is never sent to rowbo.ai, never stored by us, and never linked to an email. A public address cannot be used to move funds.

If you leave your email

Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PECR reg. 22). We store your email, the time you consented, and the non-identifying summary from your run (tax years, number of disposals, taxable total, whether it was the sample). Nothing else. It is used only to tell you about the full report launch and CARF timing. Kept until launch plus 12 months, or until you withdraw consent.

Your rights

Withdraw consent, or ask what we hold, or ask for it deleted, by emailing ben@rowbo.ai. We act within a month. You can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and tracking

No cookies. No analytics. Your browser's local storage holds a cache of daily prices so re-runs are faster; it contains no personal data and you can clear it from your browser settings.

Hosting

The site and the email form run on Vercel. Email signups are forwarded to a private rowbo.ai channel and nowhere else.

Not tax advice. taxtape applies HMRC's published cryptoasset rules (CRYPTO22000 series) mechanically to public chain data. It cannot see exchange accounts, other wallets, or your intent. Figures are indicative; check them with an accountant before filing. Rates: CGT annual exempt amount £3,000 from 2024/25; CGT at 18% / 24% from 30 October 2024.
