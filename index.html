Privacy notice

Who

TaxTape is run by rowbo.ai (Ben Rowbotham, Leicestershire, UK), the data controller. Contact: ben@rowbo.ai.

The address you paste

Sent from your browser directly to Blockscout (a public block explorer) to read its history, and to Coinbase and Frankfurter (ECB) for daily prices. It is never sent to rowbo.ai, never stored by us, and never linked to an email. A public address cannot be used to move funds.

If you leave your email

Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PECR reg. 22). We store your email, the time you consented, and the non-identifying summary from your run (tax years, number of disposals, taxable total, whether it was the sample). Nothing else. It is used only to tell you about the full report launch and CARF timing. Kept until launch plus 12 months, or until you withdraw consent.

Your rights

Withdraw consent, or ask what we hold, or ask for it deleted, by emailing ben@rowbo.ai. We act within a month. You can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and tracking

No cookies. No analytics. Your browser's local storage holds a cache of daily prices so re-runs are faster; it contains no personal data and you can clear it from your browser settings.

Hosting

The site and the email form run on Vercel. Email signups are forwarded to a private rowbo.ai channel and nowhere else.

Not tax advice. TaxTape applies HMRC's published cryptoasset rules (CRYPTO22000 series) mechanically to public chain data. It cannot see exchange accounts, other wallets, or your intent. Figures are indicative; check them with an accountant before filing. Rates: CGT annual exempt amount £3,000 from 2024/25; CGT at 18% / 24% from 30 October 2024.

Data: Blockscout (chain), Coinbase daily GBP closes (ETH, BTC), Frankfurter ECB rates (USD stablecoins). Daily closes are a valuation method HMRC accepts if used consistently.
